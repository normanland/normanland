## `> whoami`

I'm **Mayil Huseynov**, an **Operations Coordinator and Junior Data Analyst** with an academic background in **Mechatronics & Robotics Engineering**.

I work where **data, operations and problem-solving** intersect.

My interest is not limited to writing SQL queries or creating dashboards. I want to understand the system behind the numbers:

```text
what happened?
      ↓
why did it happen?
      ↓
does it matter?
      ↓
what should happen next?
```

That means moving from raw data to something that can actually support a decision.
